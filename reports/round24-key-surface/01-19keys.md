## 1.3 what this table does NOT claim

* no sizes: DTS has no per-key size; length comes from
  `key_info_query 0x37e74c0c` (device tell/query at runtime), not from DTS.
* no `key-encrypt` semantics: field parsed (`entry+0x40`, `<=0xf`) but
  empty on this DTS; effect is print+hash/select logic in secukey paths,
  secure-side meaning UNPROVEN.
* `+0x58` unknown (read by `0x37e75524`, writer not in parser).
* fixed HDCP2 names (`hdcp2lc128`, `hdcp2key`, `hdcp2`) are NOT DTS keys;
  they live in rodata (`0x37ed74f4/0x37ed7535/0x37ed72f7`) and are handled
  in 06. do not add them as rows 19..21.
